I am keeping busy this summer with projects and many daytrips! How do you prefer spending your summer?
<!--more-->

Recently I switched music programs from Ableton Live to Reaper. I am very impressed with how much you can customize with Reaper. I can map any keyboard shortcuts I want for example, it's an incredible feature. 

I have also visited my parents in northern Sweden and unexpectedly found my long lost old game consoles - Super Nintendo and Playstation 1. It was the best birthday gift I could have asked for. As the true nerd I am, I've started refurbishing them, it's really satisfying getting rid of all of that 20 year old dust. Astonishingly, both consoles work, even though they have been in uninsulated storage for so many years! Now I just need an old TV so I can play the games for fifteen minutes before I lose interest... 

I am also continuing this {% include post-link.html link="https://quii.gitbook.io/learn-go-with-tests" text="guide to Go" %} - truly an incredible resource. In addition to teaching Go syntax, it provides much needed insight into test-driven development.

I obviously like having a lot of projects going - it is relaxing to do a little bit everyday, along with fun activities, family and friends.
